---
title: "4. Restore a VM"
---

- Go to VM >> select the VM >> Entire VM Restore

![BaaS](/images/baas/baas_13_p8.png)

- Select a Restore Point

![BaaS](/images/baas/baas_14_p9.png)

- Choose the Restore mode: overwrite the original VM, or restore as a different VM.

![BaaS](/images/baas/baas_15_p9.png)

- Follow the restore. Go to VM >> History

![BaaS](/images/baas/baas_16_p10.png)

- Follow the process

![BaaS](/images/baas/baas_17_p10.png)
