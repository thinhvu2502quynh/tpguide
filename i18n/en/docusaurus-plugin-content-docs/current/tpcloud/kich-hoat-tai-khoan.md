---
title: "Activate the account and MFA"
---

TPCOMS sends an email to the address the customer provided. The message includes the user name and a sign-in token.

- Open the URL in the email. Change the password on first use, then continue to the sign-in page.
- Select your domain: Customers.
- Enter the user name and password, then click Sign in.

![TPCLOUD](/images/tpcloud/identity/p1_2.png)

- After signing in, open the Apps tab. The cards there open the cloud administration site. Click a card to sign in automatically.
- The first sign-in requires MFA. Scan the QR code with any authenticator app (for example Google Authenticator), then enter the 6-digit passcode.
